Tone Focus: 2, 3 and 4

Look at the tone mark before reading the syllable. This short drill separates the three shapes she currently mixes up.

2nd tone · rising
á ↗

Start in the middle and move up, like asking a question.

3rd tone · dip
ǎ ↘↗

Go down first, then turn upward. Find the little “v” mark.

4th tone · falling
à ↘

Start high and fall quickly, like a firm instruction.

Parent note: accuracy matters more than speed. Ask her to trace the direction in the air before choosing.
