## Scope and mode

The `#/reading` family Reading Journal. Visitor mode: Operate.

## Audience, job, and task

A Primary 4 learner records paper-book reading and practises evidence-based thinking. An adult uses the same shared library with a separate progress record and a more reflective prompt style. The parent reviews the learner's answers and confirms the learning state.

## Content and constraints

The system tracks books, chapters, optional pages and reading reflection. PDF/EPUB files are background evidence only and never become an in-app reader. Cover and copyright-page OCR proposes metadata that a person confirms. Learner-facing copy is English. Missing evidence must fall back visibly to a general prompt.

## Chosen direction

Open Book Desk: Current Book and the one clear Log Reading action sit on the left; the seven-day reading trail and thinking focus sit on the right. Library, weekly reflection and history continue as one deliberate reading flow below. Selected structure, concept seed `4c5bbc49`.

## Memorable moment

After recording a chapter, the learner immediately receives one grounded question that asks for a core idea and evidence rather than plot recall.

## Surface contract

- THESIS: Reading progress matters when it preserves both what was read and how the reader thought about it.
- OWN-WORLD: Calm Study Desk surfaces arranged as an open paper spread, with Compass Blue reserved for the current action.
- STORY: Choose a book, log a chapter, answer one serious question, then return at chapter, book and weekly milestones.
- FIRST VIEWPORT: Reading identity and profile, Current Book with Log Reading, and the seven-day thinking trail.
- FORM: Open Book Desk, selected structure, seed `4c5bbc49`.

## Quality bar

The surface must feel as quiet and legible as the established Home/Today experience while making book identity, progress and source-grounding unmistakable. It must not resemble an ebook reader, streak dashboard or school worksheet.
